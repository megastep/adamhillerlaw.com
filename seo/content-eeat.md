## What Is Working

- `/about` is the strongest page on the site and the best E-E-A-T asset.
- The site shows real business identity: Wilmington address, Delaware phone number, attorney name, and a consistent firm identity.
- `/mediation` includes a meaningful trust signal: Adam Hiller is on the Delaware Bankruptcy Court mediator register.

## Findings

### High

1. The homepage is too thin for a YMYL legal-service page. It does not adequately explain who the firm serves, what problems it solves, or why a user should contact the firm.
2. Search intent coverage is weak on the most important pages. The site does not answer enough practical questions about services, jurisdictions, fit, or process.
3. CTA language is weak. "Tell Me More" is not a strong legal-service CTA.
4. Page-level metadata is generic across the audited pages, which weakens topical relevance.

### Medium

1. E-E-A-T is uneven: the credentials exist, but most of them are trapped on `/about` instead of reinforcing the homepage and service pages.
2. Local-service framing is present but underused. The site references Delaware, Wilmington, and court experience, but not strongly enough for searchers comparing firms.
3. `/mediation` has credibility but still lacks depth around process, dispute types, who hires him, and what parties should expect.
4. `/community` helps trust a bit, but as content it is thin and not very search-useful.
5. `/links` is practitioner-friendly but not especially helpful for prospective clients.

### Low

1. Author/credential presentation could be packaged more visibly across the site.
2. Freshness signals are weak. No visible updated dates or recent examples were found.

## Why This Matters

Legal content sits squarely in YMYL territory. The site already has authentic expertise signals, but they are not being deployed where Google and users need them most. That makes this more of a packaging and site-architecture problem than a trust problem.

## Recommended Content Actions

1. Expand the homepage to at least the reference threshold and make it service-led.
2. Build dedicated practice-area pages from the existing matter list on `/about`.
3. Turn `/mediation` into a fuller service page with a stronger CTA.
4. Surface attorney credentials, admissions, and Delaware-specific experience on conversion pages.
5. Rewrite page descriptions to be unique, topical, and action-oriented.
